The Loop — yin & yang membranes

Two rings of 48 phase oscillators chase conjugate traveling waves. Each membrane is pulled toward its own pattern and toward its partner: yin reads yang's present shape error, yang reads yin's predicted next state — the asymmetric “tic”. Dot color is tracking error: cyan = locked, red = lost. Destroy yang and watch whether yin can resurrect it through the loop.

Crystallization — watching learning happen

A stream of noisy observations — three Bell-state patterns in 8 dimensions, arriving with frequencies 0.6 / 0.3 / 0.1 — is folded into a memory matrix by Hebbian updates, W ← (1−α)W + α|x⟩⟨x|. The spectrum of W is eigendecomposed live. Crystallization is not “entropy falls”: watch the top-3 eigenvalues separate, the subspace fidelity climb, and the leftover residue turn white — structure extracted, noise left behind.

The Gate — a membrane that can refuse

Four patterns live in the library: GHZ, W, |000⟩, |+++⟩. A probe is accepted only if its best overlap clears a threshold τ. First calibrate: 200 labeled probes (half noisy library members, half random strangers) sweep τ to maximize Youden's J — then τ freezes. Stream probes and watch the gate decide; raise σ to 0.5 and feel distribution shift.
